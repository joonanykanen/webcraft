    How to play

    Movement

    • WASD Move
    • Space Jump / swim up
    • W×2, Caps Lock or Ctrl+W Sprint
    • Shift Sneak (won't walk off edges)
    • Space×2 Toggle flight (creative)
    • Space/Shift Fly up/down
    • E Inventory & crafting
    • Esc Pause, then Esc again to resume
    • Click Hand the mouse to the world (cursor hides; menus get it back)

    Building

    • LMB hold Mine (per-block break time)
    • RMB Place selected block
    • MMB Pick block (creative)
    • 1–9 / wheel Hotbar
    • Q Drop selected stack
    • F3 Debug overlay
    • F Toggle hand item view
    • R Reload current world slot

    Survival tips

    • Punch trees → planks → crafting table → wooden pickaxe.
    • Stone drops cobblestone. Craft torches from coal + sticks.
    • Better tools mine faster; some ores need a better pickaxe.
    • Watch hearts & hunger. Apples drop from leaves.
    • Hostile mobs appear in the dark at night — light up your base.
    • The next goal sits above the hotbar; Milestones lists the whole chain.
    • Autosave runs every 30 s; the world survives a refresh.

    About

    WebCraft is an original voxel sandbox: an infinite procedural world, mining, building, crafting, day/night lighting and survival — running entirely client-side on WebGL 2.

    • All textures, sounds and music are generated procedurally in your browser. No third-party assets.
    • World generation is fully deterministic: a seed gives the identical world on every browser.
    • Worlds are stored in IndexedDB as a seed + block diff, and can be exported as .webcraft.json.
    • No tracking, no network calls, no account. Everything stays on your machine.

    Not affiliated with, and not derived from, any commercial voxel game.
